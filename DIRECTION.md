# Design direction — ADDERLEY MOSS

**The idea: the website is a legal instrument.** Law firms tend to sell with marble, gavels and handshakes. This one sells the thing clients actually fear, the fine print, by redlining it into plain English in front of them.

## Look
- **Paper and ink.** Paper `#f4f2ec`, ink `#0d0d0c`, and one red: the correction pen, `#d32a1f`. Blue `#1f3a8a` is used only as a rare "filed" stamp.
- **Hairlines, not boxes.** Rules, margins and clause numbers. Sections are numbered like a contract (§1, §2…), and a red margin line runs down the page.
- **Type.**

  | Typeface | Role |
  |---|---|
  | Libre Caslon Display | The voice |
  | EB Garamond italic | Asides and margin notes |
  | Schibsted Grotesk | Body text |
  | Courier Prime | Clause numbers, references, money — the record |
  | Mrs Saint Delafield | Signatures only |

- **Motion with meaning.**
  - Strike-throughs draw across the legalese and plain English types in after them.
  - Tracked changes accept themselves on scroll.
  - Signatures write themselves in.
  - The hero's 3D wax seal drops and stamps.
  - Nothing moves just to decorate. All of it respects reduced motion.
- **Buttons are pills.** Red-gradient pills with a pressed inner highlight and a soft shadow, plus ink, outline and paper variants.

## Console
The same language, made dense:
- A black sidebar with a red marker on the active item.
- Courier for every number.
- Stage steppers drawn as a ruled table.
- Conflict hits styled as margin notes.
- A running clock in the top bar, so the most valuable habit, recording time, is always one tap away.

## Client portal
It reads like a letter from the firm:
- Opens with **"Waiting on you"**: things to sign, upload or pay, each marked with the red margin rule.
- Then each matter's progress, by its real stages.
